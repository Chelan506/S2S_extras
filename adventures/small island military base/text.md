## PLUNDER OF THE GOLDEN SANDS

### ADVENTURERS NEEDED FOR ARTIFACT RECOVERY

My true name is not to be shared with your ilk, but you may call me Abdul. I am a collector of artifacts from the olden days of the Dragon Wars, and I believe I have located a truly ancient specimen. However, it is well guarded (as such specimens usually are); and the entire island of Vaidehi is deemed off limits by Her Eternal Majesty except for official purposes. Naturally, this represents an increased risk regarding the use of domestic swords for hire. As such, I seek a foreign mercenary guild willing to delve into the depths of the earth, recover the artifact, and deliver it discreetly to the dropoff point in Suncrest. Should you accept, you will receive a dossier with all the information I have on the artifact.

As you know, things would not go well for you if the God-Empress were to learn that you had ever set foot on the island. Discretion is absolutely necessary for this mission. Above all else, you must not be captured alive, and you must not leave behind any evidence connecting your presence on Vaidehi to the Mercenary Guild of Ulfos. If you fail at this, the consequences for everyone you have ever loved will be just as dire as the consequences for yourself. I have adjusted your payment for this risk accordingly.

Reward:
8000 GP split equally among the party

## QUEST DOSSIER

### The Target

Your target is an Adakonian magic artifact, believed to be entombed in a site known as Sol Ergastulum. The artifact is shaped like a steel surgical saw. It appears mundane, but it can be dangerous in the wrong hands. You should secure it quickly and try to prevent it from being used. The power of the artifact being used in the wrong circumstances may compromise the mission. Bring it back to your guild base. 

### Vaidehi

Vaidehi is primarily a military base for elite soldiers. A map of the island is attached. Difficult terrain has been marked. 

The island is off limits to anyone who isn't on official Solari business. The coast is patrolled regularly by gunboats. You will have to find some way to get on and off the island yourself. I have provided 10% of your payment (800 GP) in advance to help with this. The land is also regularly patrolled. Your job will be easiest if you avoid detection entirely.

**Vaidehi Kili.** This is the military base on Vaidehi. There are many soldiers here. Going near here would be dangerous.

**Port.** This port is used for official Solari business only. It would be difficult to make a landing here.

**Venta Brigantum.** An abandoned town.

**Sol Ergastulum**. The target site. It is an underground facility long since abandoned. There may be soldiers or archeologists nearby. Expect to have to break in. 

**House Muzumdar**. A scientific facility. The flora and fauna of Vaidehi are under active study.

**Abandoned Port.** A port that has not seen use since the Dragon Wars. 

**Venta Aerarium.** Another underground facility. There may be soldiers or archeologists nearby.

**Venta Aerarium**

A site much more thouroughly excavated than Sol Ergastulum.

In the excavated area, there are a lot of artifacts of no market value, but also 14 tarnished platinum pieces. These have been carefully placed in trays and then in crates to preserve them.

The non-excavated area lays behind a trapped door that has been fenced off for safety. Same trap as Sol Ergastulum. There are 16 more tarnished platinum pieces and a +2 shortsword back there.

One of the archaologists, Kanti Parikh, will be there sorting through the remains.

---

**Trapped Doors**

The doors have a latch and a hidden button on the side. Opening the latch without pressing the hidden button at the same time will trigger the trap.

When the trap is trigged, spring loaded spikes will shoot down from the ceiling in the 15ft in front of the door. Each creature in this area must make a DC 16 dexterity save. On a failure, a creature takes 2d6 piercing damage and is restrained. On a success, they take half that damage and are not restrained. Breaking the restraint requires breaking off the spike.

Spike: 5HP, 12AC, 4 DT
Can be lifted up with a DC10 athletics, disadvantage if lifting it off yourself.

DC 12 perception/investigation to notice the spike holes. DC 16 investigation to find the hidden button
