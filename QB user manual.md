# QuestBot User Manual

Congratulations on acquiring your new and improved QuestBot! You have been selected as one of our beta testers to help us improve our weapons technology and eventually push back the endless hordes of Kharzugan dragon riders slaughtering our people and burning our villages!!!! Make sure to take lots of notes on how your QuestBot performs so we can iron out any issues.

## Starting Your QuestBot

To start your QuestBot, locate the hidden button on the underside of your QuestBot's forward sensory apparatus. Hold the button down firmly for two seconds. It will emit a start tone and then prompt for a nickname.

### Assigning a Nickname

Interacting with your QuestBot's patent pending verbal recognition algorithm requires calling it by name. All QuestBot models respond to the names "Questbot" or "QB", but you can also assign a nickname to help you bond with your QuestBot! Be warned, the nickname will be wiped upon restart.

### Assigning Guild Members and Allies

By default, your QuestBot will use its patent pending facial recognition algorithm to identify members of your guild, information which is used in deciding task prioritization. If you would like to add a new face to the guild database, you can do so with the prompt "QuestBot: Register Guild Member". Persons added this way will remain in the database until restart.

## Commands

Your QuestBot can carry out a variety of simple tasks at your command. Simply say your QuestBot's name and then the name of the command. You can indicate a target verbal, by pointing, or by using the included infrared laser pointer.

* HEAL: administer basic first aid to a creature
* OPERATE: use built in surgical instruments and state of the art biomodelling technology to save a dying creature
* POTION: administer a potion. By default a healing potion is administered, but a different potion may be specified as an argument.
* ATTACK: attack a creature or object. Weapon may be specified as an argument if desired.
* COVER: attack any creature who enters a designated space. Use with caution; this command bypasses firing authorization.
* GOTO: move to a location
* STOP: stay in place
* FOLLOW: follow a creature
* SWITCH: activate a button or lever
* FETCH: acquire an object

## Routines

Your QuestBot has several built in routines to provide a smooth user experience for adventurers who don't have time to manage every action of their QuestBot. Each routine defines a set of actions to be followed ordered by priority. Switching between routines as the situation demands is the key to a smooth QuestBot experience. 

### Idle
1) Follow commands

### Alert

1) Scan surrounding areas for threats
2) If a threat is spotted, emit a warning tone and prompt routine change
3) Provide medical assistance to injured guild members

### Defend

1) Provide medical assistance to injured guild members
2) Stay near guild members; interpose self between guild members and threats
3) Target threats, acquiring authorization before firing

### Attack

1) Provide medical assistance to DOWNED guild members
2) Stay near guild members; interpose self between guild members and threats
3) Target threats, acquiring authorization before firing
4) Provide medical assistance to injured guild members

## Configuration

Config settings can be changed by the "Change Setting" prompt. 

### Auto-attack

DESCRIPTION: allows QuestBot to attack a creature who makes an attack against a guild member. WARNING: this bypasses firing authorization! Spellcasting currently not supported.
DEFAULT: true

### Auto-heal

DESCRIPTION: allows QuestBot to heal non-hostile non-guild member creatures automatically, when no guild members are threatened. Useful for publicity stunts!
DEFAULT: false

### Silent

DESCRIPTION: going on a stealth mission? QuestBot can play along! Silences all audio output.
DEFAULT: false


## Troubleshooting

**How do I use my QuestBot if I can't talk?**

Mute? Trapped into an antisound field? Communicating with your **QuestBot** over extreme distances? Not to worry! Your **QuestBot** comes equipped preset Handspeak commands. See Appendix B for Handspeak commands and instructions on registering new commands.
